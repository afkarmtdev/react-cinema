The revamped CineReact, screen by screen

A hand-drawn mockup of every screen in the library version: films, series, and books, typed in by you, scored out of 10, synced through PocketBase. The data on the phones is example data. Each phone scrolls, so you can see the whole screen.

CINEREACT

Login → Library → Add entry → Entry detail  ·  tabs: Library, Diary, Me

What is not drawn here
  • The Signup screen (same layout as Login with a Name field), the splash screen, and the loading, error, and empty states.
  • The delete confirmation on the edit form: the Delete link turns into Cancel and "Yes, delete it" buttons.
  • Bahasa Melayu. Every string on these screens has a Malay version.
  • A perfect 10, once confessed, shows as a yellow score pill on the card and in the diary.

Changes to this mockup

Newest first. Each entry says what changed in the app and which phones above were redrawn. The source is preview/screens.html in the repo; the screens-artifact skill has the update steps.

  1. v610 Sep 2026
    • The bottom tabs are now a floating pill: a detached capsule above the content with a tinted capsule behind the active tab, which slides to the new tab on a switch. The add button sits above it and the lists leave room under their last row. The pill hides on Entry detail and Add entry. Redrawn: Library, Diary, Me.
  2. v510 Sep 2026
    • Entries carry a Started date and a Finished date, picked on the system calendar, so old reads and watches can be backlogged on the day they happened. Finished proposes today when the status becomes done. Redrawn: Add entry (both date fields, with the status moved to Read), Entry detail (the two dates under the score).
    • The Diary opens with two cards, This month and This year: how many were finished, split into films, series, and books, and the average score. Redrawn: Diary.
    • Under the hood the project moved to Expo SDK 57 and the device library to SQLite. Neither changes what the screens show.
  3. v49 Sep 2026
    • Storage is now a runtime setting instead of a build-time env value: pick This device or PocketBase server, enter the address, Test connection, Switch storage. Redrawn: Me (Advanced group with the sign-out confirmation), Login (the Advanced link, drawn expanded, with the address field and a successful test).
    • The env value only sets the first-launch default now; the saved choice wins after that.
  4. v39 Sep 2026
    • Me gains a Settings group with an Appearance picker: Cinema, Paperback, Vice City. Redrawn: Me.
    • Every other screen now follows the chosen theme in the app. The phones here stay in Cinema; the theme board artifact shows the other two palettes on the Library and detail screens.
  5. v29 Sep 2026
    • Ratings are now a score from 1.0 to 10.0 in tenths instead of five stars. Redrawn: Library cards, Add entry, Entry detail, Diary, Me.
    • Add entry shows the warning that appears when someone taps 10, with its two answers.
    • This change log added; the eyebrow at the top now carries the version.
  6. v19 Sep 2026
    • First mockup of the library revamp: Login, Library, Filters, Add entry, Fill from OMDb, Entry detail, Diary, Me.
    • Me shows the Storage line for PocketBase sync, which landed in the code the same day.
